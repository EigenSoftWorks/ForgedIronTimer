# Forged Iron Timer Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**. I read every message.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

- your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version (Settings › General › About),
- the workout type and settings you were using,
- what you expected and what happened instead.

You'll also find tips for every workout type in the app under **Help**.

## Common questions

**Does the timer keep running if I lock my phone or switch apps?**
Yes. Workouts and rests keep running, and the beeps and voice cues keep playing in the background, so you can change songs or lock your phone.

**How do I see the timer on my Lock Screen?**
Turn on **Lock Screen & Dynamic Island** (on iPad, **Lock Screen**) in the app's Settings. The next workout you start will appear there. If iOS asks whether to allow Live Activities from Forged Iron, tap Allow. If it still doesn't appear, open the Settings app › Forged Iron and make sure Live Activities are on.

**Why doesn't the Lock Screen show the name of the current step?**
iOS doesn't let apps update the Lock Screen while they're in the background, so the app shows a bar for every step that fills on its own instead. Listen for the beeps or voice cues to know when a step changes.

**Will the beeps play if my phone is on silent?**
Yes. Cues play even with the Silent switch on, so you don't miss a round mid-workout. Use the volume buttons or turn Sound off in Settings if you want quiet.

**Will my music keep playing?**
Yes. Cues play alongside your music. With **Lower music during cues** on, your music dips briefly for each cue.

**What happens if I get a phone call during a workout?**
The timer keeps running, and the beeps and voice stay quiet while the call is ringing and while you're on it.

**Does the app need an account or internet connection?**
No. Everything works offline, and there's no account.

**How do I move my workouts to a new phone?**
Your saved workouts and Workout Log are stored only on your device. To move them, share each saved workout as a file, and export the Workout Log as a log file (in the Workout Log's ··· menu), then open those files on the new phone.

**I deleted the app. Can I get my log back?**
Only if you exported it first. Because nothing is stored on a server, deleting the app deletes its data.
