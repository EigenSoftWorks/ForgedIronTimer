The short version

Forged Iron Timer doesn't collect, store, or share any of your personal information. Everything you create stays on your device, and we have no way to see it.

Forged Iron Timer ("Forged Iron") is a workout timer for iPhone and iPad made by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What the app stores, and where

Forged Iron saves your settings, your saved workouts, and your Workout Log only on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers, no accounts, and no database of users.

If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it. You can also clear the Workout Log at any time in the app's Settings.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Forged Iron contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code (SDKs) of any kind. The app does not connect to the internet.

Spoken cues like "Go" and "Rest" are produced by your device's built-in voice, on the device.

Lock Screen display

If you turn on the Lock Screen option in the app's Settings, iOS shows your workout's progress on the Lock Screen (and, on iPhone, in the Dynamic Island) using a Live Activity. This is handled entirely on your device. The option is off until you turn it on, and iOS may ask whether to allow it the first time.

Sharing and importing

You can choose to share a saved workout or export your Workout Log as a file. This only happens when you tap Share, and the file goes wherever you choose to send it (for example Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or Files). Those apps and services handle the file under their own privacy policies.

  • A shared workout file contains the workout's name and settings.
  • An exported log contains the entries you've recorded, including any notes.

When you open a Forged Iron file someone sent you, the app reads it on your device and shows it to you before you choose to add it.

Permissions

Forged Iron works fully without any permissions. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, health data, microphone, camera, or anything else. If a future feature ever needs a permission, the app will only ask when you use that feature, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Apple

Forged Iron is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information about your download and use of apps under Apple's own privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics. These contain no personal information.

Children

Forged Iron collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Any change that affects your information will also be described in the app's update notes.
