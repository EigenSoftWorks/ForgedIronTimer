# Forged Iron Timer

A feature-rich, bold, and free workout timer for iPhone and iPad.

EMOM, Tabata, Countdown (AMRAP), For Time, custom workouts, a rest timer, and a stopwatch, with clear beeps and voice cues, a Workout Log, and big, high-contrast screens you can read from across the gym.
